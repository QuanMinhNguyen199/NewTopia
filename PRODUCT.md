## Stack

## Product Purpose
Reproduce the Vietnamese document search interface in the supplied two-page PDF as closely as possible.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Frontend recreation. No backend, authentication provider, document database, or LLM credentials were supplied. Initial content is a visual fixture transcribed from the PDF, not verified legal information. Local interactions must identify this limitation rather than fabricate search answers.
## Brand Commitments
The PDF is the binding visual reference: dark background, narrow centered column, compact typography, blue actions and citations, document lists and source excerpts.
